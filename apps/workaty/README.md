## How to add a Blog Post with TinaCMS

1. Open the Admin Panel
   Start your local development server and open your browser to `http://localhost:3000/admin.`

2. Navigate to Blog Section
   In the sidebar, go to `Blog Posts`, then click `Add Files` to create a new post.

3. Fill in Your Content
   Enter your title, upload a cover image, write a short excerpt, and start creating your blog content.

4. Save & Publish
   Once you're done, hit `Save` — your post will be instantly updated and visible on the site (based on your Tina setup).
